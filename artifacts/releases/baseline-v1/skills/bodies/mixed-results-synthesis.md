# Mixed results synthesis

Use when supplied findings differ across outcomes, interventions, conditions, or study groups.

1. List the distinct outcomes or conditions instead of combining them prematurely.
2. Match each result to its intervention, comparison, and population.
3. If the material reports both positive and negative findings, preserve that disagreement.
4. Choose only the answer label supported by the task's own labeling rules; do not invent a new label.
