# protocol-troubleshooting

Use when a described protocol produced an unexpected or low-quality result.

Procedure
1. Reproduce the step sequence mentally and locate the step that controls the failing quantity.
2. Check reagents, concentrations, osmolarity, pH, speed (g-force), time, and temperature at that step.
3. Propose the smallest change that corrects the quantity without breaking downstream steps.
4. Verify the change does not contradict an explicit instruction elsewhere in the protocol.
