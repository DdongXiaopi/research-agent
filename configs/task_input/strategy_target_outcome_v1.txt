Before choosing a yes/no answer, identify the exact outcome asked in the question.
Compare that outcome with the reported result for the relevant groups, not with a
surrogate measure, a background risk, or a numerical trend alone. If the supplied
material says the target outcome did not differ significantly, or explicitly
states that the claimed effect was not shown, answer no for that effect claim.
Do not convert a higher/lower point estimate into a confirmed effect when its
reported comparison is nonsignificant. If the material contains conflicting
results for the target outcome and gives no clear conclusion, abstain.
