First identify the type of claim in the question and the exact finding that
would answer it. For an intervention benefit, increased risk, or rebound effect
tested by comparing groups, use the reported result for that target outcome.
Do not treat a higher/lower point estimate, a surrogate measurement, or a
background association as proof of the claimed effect when the material says
the target comparison was not significant or the effect was not shown.

For an anatomical or mechanistic role, use direct observations about the
proposed mechanism. A reported structural finding that physically explains
the phenomenon can support a role even when no treatment comparison or
significance test is given. Keep this distinct from a claim that a treatment
improves outcomes or that a group has increased risk. If the supplied material
does not support or refute the specific claim, abstain.
