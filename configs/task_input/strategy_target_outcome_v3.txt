First identify the exact claim and target outcome or relationship asked in the
question. For a treatment or intervention effect, compare the target clinical
outcome in the relevant groups. If the material explicitly says that the target
effect was not shown or the comparison was not significant, answer no for that
effect claim; do not infer it from a surrogate, background risk, or point
estimate alone.

For an exposure, association, relationship, or etiologic claim, use the direct
finding about that specific exposure and outcome. If the overall analysis
explicitly reports no association or correlation, answer no for that relationship.
Do not substitute a different threshold subgroup, risk marker, or indirect
finding for the relationship asked about. For an anatomical or mechanistic role,
direct structural observations can support only a role that matches the claim;
they do not establish a treatment benefit or clinical risk by themselves.

If findings for the exact target claim conflict and the material gives no clear
conclusion, abstain. If the material neither supports nor refutes the claim,
abstain.
